# 🛒 Vrinda Store Sales Analysis — Excel Project

## 📌 Project Overview
Analyzed annual sales data of Vrinda Store for the year 2022 using Microsoft Excel.
The goal was to identify sales trends, customer behavior, and top-performing channels to help the store grow in 2023.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Dashboard)

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed
- **Monthly Sales Trend** — Identified peak and low sales months
- **Men vs Women** — Women contributed ~64% of total sales
- **Order Status** — 93% orders successfully delivered
- **Top 5 States** — Maharashtra, Karnataka, UP, Telangana, Tamil Nadu
- **Platform Performance** — Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart are top channels
- **Age vs Gender** — Adult women are the highest buying segment

## 💡 Key Insights
- Women customers are more likely to buy compared to men
- Maharashtra, Karnataka and UP are the top 3 states
- Adult age group (30-49 years) contributes the most to sales
- Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra contribute to ~85% of total orders
